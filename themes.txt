Zombie Survival Game - character runs around and finds ingredients to create cure before zombies catch him

        - Mutation system - the plague evolves over time, changing enemy types and symptoms
        - Infection meter - player slowly gets infected; manage exposure with masks, meds, and rest
        - Plague rats - carriers that spread plague to new areas if not exterminated
        - Plague doctor NPC - mysterious masked healer who trades cures for rare herbs
        - Corrupt officials - leaders hoarding medicine; expose them or side with them
        - Burn the bodies - timed cleanup mechanic; corpses left too long spawn new infections
            - must find something to burn them with
            - timed releases - items used to burn bodies are limited and don't exist at first
        - Immunity bloodline - certain NPCs are immune; protect and escort them to scientists
        - Snake oil salesmen - fake cures circulating; test remedies to tell real from scam
        - Plague bell curfew - city shuts down at night when zombies/difficulty increase, but also loot
            - can 'sleep' instead in any 'safe house' to pass the night to morning
        - Supply caravan escort - guard medicine shipments between towns through infected territory
        - Herbal crafting - gather ingredients to brew tonics, antidotes, and preventatives
            - use real herbs and their properties to make it educational
        - Abandoned hospital dungeon - explore an overrun medical center for research notes
            - this could be the real 'finish' point of each stage, where the player finds a cure or dies to infection
            - So there is a fog of war system surrounding the city and the player must explore to find the hospital

Sub themes:
    - 80's baby! - recreate the visual style of the 80's media
    - take a chance on me - create a system of chance
    - i wish it would rain - implement weather effects of some kind

New feature ideas (building on the current game):

    Moment-to-moment play
        - Sprint/stamina - short burst of speed with a cooldown bar; zombies lunge in return
        - Push/shove - knock a zombie down 
            - short cooldown (2 secs, no visible indicator) 
            - include shoving animation toward the closest zombie
            - include getting up animations - takes 3 seconds
        - Crates actually open - herbs, a stamina tonic, a distraction item, or nothing
            - Throwable distraction - toss a bottle/rock to pull nearby zombies toward the noise
            - Ingredient clues - notes in crates, minimap glows for a second with a ping around the ingredient location
            - Demolition charge - collapses a building; the falling sprite-stack crushes everything in its
                  shadow and permanently opens a shortcut through the block (show collapsed rubble sprite/s)
                  - animation for falling building
        - Car alarms - touching a car sets off its alarm, drawing every zombie in earshot screenwide-ish (circular) radius for 4 seconds
        - Infection trail - the survivor leaves footprints the zombies can see,
          fading over 10 seconds

    City and exploration
        - Minimap - corner map that mirrors the fog of war; cure herbs ping it when close
        - Street signs / district names - 
            - each quadrant gets a name and visual flavor so
            - Street signs at intersections and district names on the minimap help
        - Interiors - a handful of buildings with an open door; step inside for possible loot but
          the vision circle becomes your only light, and there are zombies (bigger on the inside!)
        - Safe houses - a few marked buildings where zombies never follow; heal slowly
          inside, but time (and the spawner) keeps running
        - Rain (i wish it would rain) 
            - rain muffles your noise 
            - smaller zombie sight range 
            - hides their shuffling (no audio cue)
            - 30% chance during any given game
            - Rains the whole time or not.
            - Puddles slow you down and make extra noise, spawn every 5-12 seconds

    Zombies and AI
        - Zombie variants - 
            - runner (fast, fragile sight)
            - brute (slow, wide aggro, shoves you)
            - screamer (calls the horde in a large radius when it spots you), very slow
            - different sprites and audio cues, and animations. Slower zombies should move in really messed up looking ways
        - Hearing model - zombies react to running footsteps, alarms, and breaking
          crates, not just line of sight.
        - Horde events - every 30 seconds a siren sounds and a wave of 20 zombies spawns at the map
          edge and runs down one road path from one edge to another; hide or reroute

    Cure and progression
        - Brewing step (herbal crafting) - collecting all 4 ingredients isn't the win;
          carry them to the hospital/lab and survive a timed brew while zombies possibly break in through windows
        - Partial doses - 2 ingredients = a weak serum that slows infection gain for a
          while; spend progress now or bank it for the cure
        - Meta-unlocks - surviving runs unlocks starting perks (faster walk, bonus chest,
          longer vision circle) to reward replays on harder difficulties

    Zombie-player endgame
        - Zombie abilities - as the player zombie: a roar that rallies nearby NPC zombies
          to converge on the survivor - 10 second cooldown
            - a short lunge attack

    Presentation / juice
        - 80's mode (80's baby!) - neon HUD, synthwave palette swap
        - Score screen - 
            - time survived
            - herbs used
            - zombies dodged/shoved (within a certain closeness)
            - cure progress
        - Photo of the fallen - the end screen shows your explored-map
          zoomed out when you died as player, fades over to map of zombie map zoomed out when you died as zombie

    Outside the box (unexpected elements)
        - Zombie empathy - herbs can be fed to a zombie instead of eaten; a fed zombie
          calms, turns a different color, and follows you as a shield.
        - One phone call - a random payphone rings every 7-9 seconds; answering roots you in place
          (vulnerable) while a voice gives one true secret (a cure location, the next
          horde's road) - hanging up early garbles the tip
        - Zombie parkour inversion - as the player zombie you can shamble THROUGH
          buildings' ground floors (you're dead, doors mean nothing). Glass breaking
          noise or door clattering when going through.
        - Difficulty area - difficulty menu: easy/medium/hard are three 
          doors in a starting alley; walk through only one. Door one is new clean
          looking door (easy), two is a dirty dilapidated door. 3 is an ancient ruin looking door (hard).
          Door locks behind you.
        - The herbs sing - each cure herb hums a looping musical phrase that gets
          louder as you near it (audio-only navigation through fog); the four phrases
          harmonize into the game's main theme when all are collected
        - Hard is navigation by vision circle only, fog of war does not disappear.
